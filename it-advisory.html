● SCOPE

What this covers, and what it doesn't

This is advisory, not support. Being clear about the line matters more here than anywhere else on the site.

In scope

  • Security policy and risk decisions
  • Vendor and provider evaluation
  • Tech budget and roadmap planning
  • Incident response planning (before something happens)
  • Cyber insurance and compliance questionnaire review
  • Translating between leadership and your IT provider

Not this

  • Help desk or day-to-day IT support
  • Managed services / MSP work
  • Monitoring workstations or servers
  • Troubleshooting individual user issues
● WHO THIS IS FOR

Leadership without a technical peer

You've got an IT person or an MSP handling the day-to-day, but no one whose job it is to step back and ask whether the overall setup, spend, and risk actually make sense. That's this.
